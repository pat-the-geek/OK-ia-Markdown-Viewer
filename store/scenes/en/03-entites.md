# Lakes and peaks — summer season review

> Visitor numbers at Swiss tourist sites, June to September.

The summer season closes with visitor numbers up **9%**. Around Lake Geneva, Lausanne, Montreux and Vevey welcomed most visitors, ahead of the lakeside promenades of Geneva.

In the mountains, Zermatt and Grindelwald were full from July, and the Jungfrau draws ever more hikers.

In Central Switzerland, Lucerne benefits from the return of cruises on Lake Lucerne. South of the Alps, Lugano and Lake Lugano confirm their appeal, while St. Moritz bets on summer as much as on winter. Finally, Bern gains weekend visitors.

| Region | Overnight stays | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Lake Geneva region | 1,240,000 | +11% |
| Valais and Bernese Alps | 980,000 | +8% |
| Central Switzerland | 760,000 | +7% |
| Ticino and Graubünden | 690,000 | +9% |
