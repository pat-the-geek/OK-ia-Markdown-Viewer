# Trail guide 2027

## Twelve routes between lakes and mountains

![The Säntis](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/S%C3%A4ntis%20-%20Batzberg%20IMG%209803.JPG?width=1200)

The 2027 guide brings together twelve marked routes, from the lakeshores to the ridges of the Prealps. Each gives its duration, its elevation gain and the public transport at the start and finish.

| Route | Duration | Elevation gain |
|---|---|---|
| Lakeshore | 2 h 30 | 120 m |
| Terraced vineyards | 3 h | 380 m |
| Prealpine ridges | 5 h | 950 m |

## Practical tips

Set off early in summer, check the mountain weather and take water: several routes pass no fountain for long stretches.
