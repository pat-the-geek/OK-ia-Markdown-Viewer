# Annual report 2026 — Regional tourist office

## A record year by the lake

![Chillon Castle](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Castle%20of%20Chillon%20N.jpg?width=1200)

2026 will be remembered as the year of records: **2.1 million overnight stays**, 12% more than in 2025. The growth comes first from Swiss visitors, then from European markets, which are back to their pre-pandemic level.

## Overnight stays by season

| Season | Overnight stays | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | 410,000 | +8% |
| Summer | 920,000 | +14% |
| Autumn | 470,000 | +11% |
| Winter | 300,000 | +9% |

## The most visited sites

![Vevey](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Vevey%2C%20Switzerland.jpg?width=1200)

The promenades of Vevey and Montreux, Chillon Castle and the terraced vineyards remain visitors' favourite sites. Lake cruises are up 18%.

## Outlook

The office is preparing three projects for 2027: a single ticket for transport and museums, new marked trails through the vineyards, and a welcome in seven languages.
