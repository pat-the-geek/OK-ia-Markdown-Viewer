# Swiss cities for a weekend

![Bern from the air](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Bern%20luftaufnahme.png?width=1200)

Short city breaks are growing: Bern, Zurich and Basel lead, followed by Lucerne. Visitors happily combine a city and a lake — Lake Zurich, Lake Neuchâtel or Lake Thun.
