# Lakes and peaks — summer season review

![Lake Lucerne](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Vierwaldst%C3%A4ttersee.jpg?width=1200)

> Visitor numbers at Swiss tourist sites, June to September.

The summer season closes with visitor numbers up **9%**. Around Lake Geneva, Lausanne, Montreux and Vevey welcomed most visitors, ahead of Geneva. In the mountains, Zermatt and Grindelwald were full from July, and the Jungfrau draws ever more hikers.
