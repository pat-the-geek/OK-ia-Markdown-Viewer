# Ticino along the water

![Lugano seen from Sighignola](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Lugano%20from%20Sighignola.jpg?width=1200)

Lugano, Locarno and Ascona make the most of a mild autumn: terraces stay open until early November. Bellinzona, with its three castles, draws more families, while Lake Lugano gets its evening boats back.
