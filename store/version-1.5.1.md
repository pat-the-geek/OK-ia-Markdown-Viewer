## English (en-US)

### Promotional text

```
New: present with your iPhone as a remote, your notes and the colour of time. And for everyone: formulas, ePub and slides as images.
```

### What's New
```
Present, and find your vault everywhere.

NEW FOR EVERYONE
• Slides as images: one image per slide, in 16:9, square or portrait, ready for a carousel.
• Mathematical formulas, written as on GitHub and in Obsidian ($…$ and $$…$$), when reading, in the slideshow and in Word, PowerPoint and ePub exports.
• An ePub export, to read your reports in Books, with the table of contents, images, diagrams and maps.
• The estimated length of a slideshow, based on its notes.
• Images and illustrations carry into Word, PowerPoint and PDF exports, and links in the PDF can be tapped.
• A vault that explains itself to assistants: on request, md Viewer writes an AGENTS.md file and an index into the vault, so Claude Code, Codex or VS Code find their way. md Viewer sends nothing.

ANALYTIC OPTION
• Speaker notes: written in the app or suggested when converting to a presentation, and carried into the PowerPoint export.
• Rehearse your slideshow, every slide timed. While presenting, the colour of time shows where you stand, without reading the timer.
• Your iPhone as a remote: advance, read your notes, draw and point on the slide of your Mac or iPad.
• A PDF handout, one to three slides per page, with or without your notes.
• Your colours: logo, font and footer, right into the PowerPoint export.
• The week in review: what came into your vault since your last visit.
• Import Excel, CSV, Pages, Keynote and Numbers too.
• Your vault in iCloud Drive, on a network share or on a disk, the same on all your devices.
• All your annotations in one list; they are written into the file itself.

If md Viewer is useful to you, an App Store review helps us a lot.
```
